Prenatal and postpartum care that comes to you.

A volunteer-run mobile clinic bringing prenatal visits, blood-pressure and diabetes screening, maternal mental-health screening, nutrition counseling, and help enrolling in Medicaid and WIC to community sites in Athens-Clarke County — and to nearby counties that have no obstetric care.

What we provide

Scheduled clinic days at trusted community sites, delivered by licensed volunteer clinicians under a medical director.

Prenatal & postpartum visits

Routine visits under written protocols; postpartum check at 2–6 weeks; telehealth follow-up between visits.

Screening

Blood pressure at every visit, urine and point-of-care glucose testing, same-day referral for any warning sign.

Mental health

Perinatal depression and anxiety screening each trimester and postpartum, with warm referral to behavioral-health partners.

Nutrition

Prenatal nutrition and weight-gain counseling, prenatal vitamins, WIC enrollment on site.

Education

Warning signs of pregnancy complications (CDC "Hear Her"), birth planning, breastfeeding, newborn care, family planning.

Navigation

Medicaid and 12-month postpartum coverage, WIC, delivery-hospital registration, transportation, doula and home-visiting referrals, Georgia Family Healthline 1-888-457-4769.

Out of scope: deliveries, diagnostic ultrasound, emergency care. In an emergency call 911.

Volunteer & training

Every volunteer completes orientation before serving. Licensed clinicians are enrolled in the Georgia Volunteer Health Care Program, which provides liability protection for volunteer care at qualifying free clinics.

Module 1 — Orientation (all volunteers, 90 min)

Why the clinic exists; our model and your role; scope and referral rules; respectful maternity care; confidentiality; urgent maternal warning signs; mental-health screening; navigation resources; clinic-day flow; documentation.

Module 2 — Clinical volunteers (4 h)

Protocols and standing orders, blood-pressure thresholds, point-of-care testing, fetal heart tones, EPDS scoring, escalation and emergency transfer, GVHCP requirements.

Module 3 — Site operations (2 h)

Setup, privacy, infection control, emergency kit, patient flow, forms custody, incident reporting.

Module 4 — Train-the-trainer

For volunteers and partner-clinic staff who will help other clinics adopt this service line.

Roles we need
